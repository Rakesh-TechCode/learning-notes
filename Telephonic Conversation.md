## 2. Salary Expectations & Negotiation

> **My salary target**
>
> | Particulars | Amount |
> |---|---:|
> | Current Fixed CTC | ₹4.85 LPA |
> | Expected CTC | ₹9 LPA |
> | Minimum Target | ₹8 LPA |
> | 30% Hike Offer | ₹6.31 LPA |

### Q6. What is your current CTC and expected CTC?

| Answer |
|---|
| Considering my 3+ years of experience in Java backend development, Spring Boot, microservices, and Kafka, I'm expecting around **9 LPA**. However, I'm open to discussion depending on the role and overall compensation structure. |

### Q7. What if HR says we can offer only a 30% hike?

<font color="red">**Important Salary Negotiation Scenario**</font>

**HR:** Your current CTC is 4.85 LPA. As per our company policy, we can offer only a 30% hike.

**Your Answer:**

| Answer |
|---|
| I understand your company's compensation policy. However, considering my experience and the technical responsibilities of this role, I was expecting something closer to **9 LPA**. Is there any flexibility in the budget? |

**Strategy:** Stop speaking after asking this question and let HR respond.

> Don't immediately reduce your expectation.

### Q8. What if HR says 30% is the maximum?

**Your Answer:**

| Answer |
|---|
| I appreciate the offer, but 6.31 LPA would be below my expected range. I'm genuinely interested in this opportunity. If we can work towards **8 LPA or above**, I'd be happy to proceed. |

**This communicates three things:**

- You respect their policy.
- You are interested in the opportunity.
- You have a clear salary expectation.

### Q9. What if HR says your current salary is too low for 9 LPA?

**Your Answer:**

| Answer |
|---|
| I understand your concern. However, I believe compensation should also reflect my experience, technical skills, and the responsibilities of the new role, rather than being based only on my current salary. I'm confident that **9 LPA** is a reasonable expectation for the opportunity I'm targeting. |

> **Tip:** Practice this answer carefully. Don't let your current CTC become the only basis for salary negotiation.

### Q10. What if HR says our maximum budget is 7 LPA?

**Your Answer:**

| Answer |
|---|
| Thank you for sharing the budget. My expectation is around **9 LPA**, and my minimum target is **8 LPA**. Is there any possibility of reconsidering the budget? I'd definitely be interested if we can get closer to that range. |

**If they cannot move beyond ₹7 LPA:**

Don't feel pressured to accept immediately. Evaluate:

- Role and technical responsibilities.
- Learning and career growth.
- Fixed pay vs. variable pay.
- Your other opportunities.

## 4. Quick Revision Checklist

Use this checklist before attending a telephonic round.

- [ ] Tell me about yourself.
- [ ] Explain your current project.
- [ ] What is your role and responsibility?
- [ ] Why are you looking for a change?
- [ ] What is your notice period?
- [ ] What is your current and expected CTC?
- [ ] How will you handle a 30% hike offer?
- [ ] What if 30% is the maximum?
- [ ] What if HR questions your 9 LPA expectation?
- [ ] What if the maximum budget is 7 LPA?
- [ ] Are you holding any offers?
- [ ] Are you comfortable with the location and work mode?

---

**Final Preparation Tip:** Don't memorize every word. Understand the key points and practice speaking naturally. Keep your answers short, confident, and consistent with your actual experience.
